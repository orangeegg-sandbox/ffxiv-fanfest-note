## Seating and standing

[Current stage viewing guidance](https://fanfest.finalfantasyxiv.com/2026/jp/stages/#viewing_guidelines), marked **2026年10月23日（金）更新**:

- **「着席エリアは全席自由席です。」** Fill front seats where available as staff direct; no assigned-seat ticket.
- **「ピアノコンサートおよびスペシャルライブを含むすべてのプログラムで、着席エリアの座席の撤去はありません。音楽ステージの間もご利用いただけます。」** Chairs remain for both concerts.
- **「スペシャルライブに限り、着席エリア内でもその場に立ち上がってご観覧いただけます。ただし、安全確保のため、通路への移動やステージ前方へ詰め寄る行為はお控えください。」** In the seated area, standing at one's own place is permitted specifically for THE PRIMALS special live. Do not generalize that exception to the piano concert. The separate standing-viewing area on the plan is distinct from standing up in a seated area.
- **「プログラムごとのお客さまの入れ替えは行いません。」** No audience turnover between programs.
- **「座席に荷物を置いたまま離席することはご遠慮ください。移動するときは必ず荷物をお持ちください。」** No saving a place by leaving bags behind.
- **「安全確保および事故防止のため、場内での折りたたみ椅子のご利用はご遠慮ください。」** No folding chairs.
- **「車いすをご利用の方は、ステージエリアにお越しの際スタッフへお声がけください。専用のスペースへご案内いたします。」** Ask staff for the wheelchair space.

The overview and ticket notice also describe screen/seating areas in other halls. They do not promise that a particular overflow location has a seat or view available at a given time; an alternative should be stated conditionally and staff-directed.

## Admission preparation and re-entry

[about#entrance](https://fanfest.finalfantasyxiv.com/2026/jp/about/#entrance):

- Tickets are supplied in the free **スクウェア・エニックス アプリ**. Install it and verify the ticket before arriving. Screenshots and printed copies are invalid: **「電子チケット画面のスクリーンショットや、電子チケットを紙に印刷したものではご入場いただけません。」**
- Each person must display their own ticket; two-ticket purchasers must distribute the companion's ticket before entry. Bring the security token/authentication app if needed to log in again.
- Bags are inspected on entry: **「入場時に手荷物検査を実施します。」**
- The issued wristband replaces the ticket for admission/re-entry. An already-used ticket alone does not suffice without the band being worn.
- **「2日通し券用のリストバンドは2日間共通です。1日目終了後も外さず、必ず2日間通しての着用をお願いいたします。途中で外された場合は無効になり、以降入場ができません。」** Keep the same wristband on across both days. Lost/damaged/stolen bands are not reissued.

[about#caution](https://fanfest.finalfantasyxiv.com/2026/jp/about/#caution) says identity may be checked and asks visitors to bring an official document identifying the purchaser. It warns that entry/exit restrictions may be imposed for crowd control and that getting in or out can take time.

## Recording, belongings and penlights — preserve scope

The general [official cautions](https://fanfest.finalfantasyxiv.com/2026/jp/about/#caution) say:

> 会場内での写真撮影・動画撮影・録音に一部制限があります。会場でのスタッフの指示に従っていただけますようお願いいたします。スタッフの指示に従わない場合、ご退場いただく場合があります。

This supports telling users to follow each area's and staff's recording instructions. **No music-specific total recording ban was found in the inspected pages**, so do not invent one or say recording is universally allowed.

The same cautions say **「周りのお客様のご迷惑となる服装、大きなお荷物を持ってのご来場はお控えください。」**, **「会場周辺での徹夜等は、固くお断りいたします。」** and **「危険物等は一切持ち込めません。」** The site does not supply a concert-specific bag-dimension limit here. Folding chairs and leaving bags on seats are specifically covered by stage guidance above. Costume headwear/wigs/accessories must not obstruct surrounding seated visitors' view, per the costume guidelines.

**Penlight scope:** the only explicit permission found is under [cinema live viewing](https://fanfest.finalfantasyxiv.com/2026/jp/stages/#live_viewing): **「声出しやペンライトの使用もOK！」** Its following explanation also permits cheering goods for the cinema's 応援上映. This is **not** a general rule for Makuhari's piano concert or THE PRIMALS stage. No on-site concert-specific penlight/glowstick size or brightness rule was found in the inspected official pages. If mentioned in the guide, say the on-site rule was not confirmed and follow staff, rather than importing cinema rules.

## Cloakroom and the Day 2 airport plan

The separate [Cross Wave Makuhari cloakroom service](https://fanfest.finalfantasyxiv.com/2026/jp/about/#cloak-room) is paid and requires its own reservation. Deposits are accepted until **19:00**, while retrieval is accepted until **21:45**; after 19:00 it is retrieval-only. The official page specifically warns:

> なお、イベントの終了間際や、ステージプログラム「ピアノコンサート」「スペシャルライブ」前は混雑が予想されます。スケジュールに余裕をもってご利用ください。

This is useful preparation advice but is not a requirement that every concert attendee book the cloakroom.

THE PRIMALS runs **19:00–20:30**. The site's existing **hypothetical November 2 00:00 Haneda flight** planning example uses the **November 1 19:15 Kaihin-Makuhari bus**; these cannot both be attended in full. Cross-link that provisional transport plan and tell the user to reconcile the actual flight with concert/luggage time. Do not claim a confirmed flight, calculate a new personal latest-safe exit time, or assume that the event's 21:00 close is the concert's finish time.
